SnappetAI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026 · Last updated: June 15, 2026

SnappetAI ("the app", "we", "us") is built around a simple principle: your data stays on your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what we do — and do not — do with it.

The short version

  • No account. No sign-up. No servers. SnappetAI has no backend. We do not have a server that receives, stores, or processes your data.
  • Everything is processed and stored locally on your iPhone, Apple Watch, and iCloud backup (if you enable it). Your workouts, heart-rate data, photos, videos, highlight reels, journal entries, habits, expenses, and budgets never leave your device except when you explicitly export or share them.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Data the app accesses (and why)

All access is requested just-in-time, with the system permission prompt, and is used only on your device for the feature you invoked:

  • Health data (Apple HealthKit) — with your permission, the app reads heart-rate, resting heart-rate, and workout data to build heart-rate-driven highlight reels, show your workout summary, and estimate effort/energy. On Apple Watch, the app may write a workout session so heart rate keeps streaming during a workout. HealthKit data is processed entirely on your device, is never transmitted off your device, and is never used for advertising or marketing, nor shared or sold to any third party.
  • Photos & videos — with your permission, the app reads photos/videos shot during a workout to assemble highlight reels, and can save a finished reel back to your library. Media is read and rendered on-device; nothing is uploaded.
  • Camera — used only if you scan a receipt in the expense tools. Images are processed on-device (text recognition) and are not uploaded.
  • Bluetooth — used only to connect to a heart-rate monitor/chest strap you choose to pair, for live heart rate during a workout.
  • Notifications — used only for workout/rest and focus-timer alerts you start.

What leaves your device — only when you choose

  • Sharing or exporting a reel, workout, or other content via the iOS share sheet (Photos, AirDrop, Messages, …). What happens then is governed by the destination you pick.
  • iCloud backup, if enabled for the device, may include the app's local data — this is Apple's encrypted backup, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Improving the app

To improve highlight selection, the app records — on your device only — which auto-generated highlights you keep, remove, pin, reorder, or export. This stays in the app's local storage, is used only on-device to tune the selection algorithm for you, and is never transmitted off your device.

Children's privacy

SnappetAI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Because there is no account and no server, the app does not collect personal information in the first place.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can also delete individual items within the app, and revoke Health/Photos/Camera/Bluetooth permissions any time in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised version at this URL.
