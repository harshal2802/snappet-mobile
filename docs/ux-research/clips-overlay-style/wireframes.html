Clips — Overlay Style

One default for every clip's heart-rate tile and title. Opened from a new ✎ button in the Clips toolbar. The default applies to feed posts, fullscreen, Share with heart rate and highlight reels, so a post and the video you share from it always match. Order of priority: a session you styled in the Studio → your default → the built-in Broadcast scorebug. The default is saved in the database, so it's included in backups.

Templates and stats are the Studio's existing ones (Broadcast, Glass Hero, HR Trace, HUD Pill, Zone Ring, Rail, Strain), so nothing new to draw. The preview is one of your own recent clips.
