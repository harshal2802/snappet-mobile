Clips — Search & Filter

Optional, zero-cost when idle: the feed looks exactly as it does today until you either pull down (revealing the standard iOS search field) or tap a filter chip. Search matches the post's climb / exercise name and session title; chips filter by Favorites ♥ · Climbs · Gym · Videos · Photos (one discipline + one media kind at a time, favorites stacks with anything). An active search/filter shows an honest “N of M posts · Clear” line, and a no-match result offers one-tap recovery. Same pattern language as the Kilter browse chips (#264): filters visible, not buried in a toolbar glyph.
