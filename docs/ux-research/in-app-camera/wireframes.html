In-app camera — like the iPhone Camera, built for sets

Today the record button opens Apple's stock picker (record / stop / flip, and it was recording at ~480×360 — fixed separately in prompt 190). A custom camera (AVFoundation) unlocks what the iPhone Camera has — lenses, 4K/60, slo-mo, tap-to-focus/exposure lock, zoom, torch, grid — plus things only Snappet can do because it knows the set: a hands-free countdown, auto-stop when you log the set, and live HR on screen. Your phone (iPhone 13 Pro Max) has 0.5× / 1× / 3× lenses, 4K60 and 240 fps slo-mo; other phones show what they have.
