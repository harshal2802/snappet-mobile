Festival — Guided getting started

A first-run onboarding for the Festival mini-app: a short value tour, then a guided setup checklist that replaces the blank "download a lineup" empty state and walks a new user through their first real actions — never blocking, always skippable.

Shape. Two parts. (1) A 3-card tour shown once on first open of the module — what the app does, in its own voice, ending in one CTA. (2) A setup checklist that stands in for the empty state and then rides as a dismissible banner on the schedule until its three steps are done: add a lineup → star a few sets → turn on reminders. Each step deep-links to a surface that already ships (prompts 02 · 04). Reuses SnappetColor.festival (UV orchid), the catalog install, the ★ plan, and set reminders — no new capability, just a guided on-ramp.

Flow B · The guided setup
A checklist instead of a blank slate — then out of the way

The tour hands off to a three-step checklist that replaces the empty state. Each step is a real action that deep-links to a shipped surface and ticks itself off when done. Once a lineup's installed it shrinks to a dismissible banner on the schedule, and disappears for good when complete or dismissed — onboarding you never have to see twice.
