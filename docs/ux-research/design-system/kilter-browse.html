What changes

  • One board card replaces the board strip, the “ON THE BOARD” rail and the arrival/confirm banners’ separate bands. It shows the board, angle and state; Connect + Start when idle; the live session (time, HR, climbs, sends) with End when on. The last-lit climb is one line inside it (All › = On the Board).
  • Tapping the board line (“Home wall 12×12 · 40°”) opens the same Board / Size / Angle menu today’s chips have; during a session, tapping the card opens the session. Same “everything opens its source” rule as Home.
  • Chips: one style everywhere (hairline outline; accent only on the selected one). Board + Angle move into the card’s board line; Filters (with Sort inside, as today), Grade range, Mine and Saved fit in row 1; My ascents stays row 2.
  • Count + Clear become the list’s small header instead of their own bar.
  • Climb rows: name in regular-medium weight (not bold headline), setter and your status on one grey line (✓ Sent green, ◐ tries amber — meaning colours stay), grade as plain text on the right with stars and ascents under it; no capsule badges.
  • Your accent, not orange: today the whole Kilter screen is tinted orange (buttons, chips, toolbar, stars). Restyled, actions use your accent like the rest of the app; orange stays only on the Kilter app icon. Stars go neutral grey.
  • No cut-off chips: today the Board chip is clipped (“Board Te…”) behind Mine / Saved; the board moves into the card so the row fits.
  • Climb of the day stays, as the first row with a small ✦ — not its own section header.
  • Title “Kilter” (was “Kilter Board”), toolbar unchanged (History, Create, More).

Unchanged

  • Every filter, sort and the Plan / Stats / On the Board / Scan / Settings entries; the climb detail screen (next, 3b); board connect / sharing behaviour; Kilter’s orange stays as the app’s icon colour only.

Then

  • 3b: climb detail · 3c: Clips feed · 3d: Gym Tracker + History · then the rest, one PR each.
