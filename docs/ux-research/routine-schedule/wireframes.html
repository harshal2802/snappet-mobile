Schedule a routine · Habits · QR share & scan

Two requests, drawn before they're built: (1) put a routine on a schedule, with detailed reminder settings and an optional link to the Habits mini-app; (2) share that routine with its schedule over QR, and scan one in from the Routines screen, either with the camera or from a photo in your library.

Already exists (reused, not rebuilt): routine QR share (snappet://routine/v1/…, deflate + base64url), the camera scanner, and the import-confirm preview. Today Scan is only reachable inside another routine's Share sheet, which is backwards. Nothing handles schedules or photo import yet.
The third request (screen locking mid-workout) is already fixed on branch fix/workout-keep-screen-awake. It needs no wireframe.

1 · Scheduling a routine Option A — per-routine schedule

Each routine owns its own schedule, set from its detail screen. The Routines list gains an Up next card and a week strip, so the plan is visible without opening anything.

Your calls

Q1 · Where schedules live. A per-routine schedule (1–5) · B one weekly plan (5B) · A now, B later. My pick: A now, B later. A covers every repeat pattern, travels over QR, and gives each routine its own reminders; B can be layered on top once A exists.
Q2 · Habit per routine, or shared? The editor (3) lets you pick either: a new habit per routine, or attach several routines to one "Gym" habit. Default: a new habit per routine, with "attach to existing" one tap away.
Q3 · Streaks on scheduled habits. Count scheduled days only (rest days neither help nor break the streak) vs. keep the current every-day streak. My pick: scheduled days only. Otherwise a Mon/Wed/Fri routine can never have a streak above 1.
Q4 · "Skip today" in Habits. Excused (streak survives) vs. missed (streak breaks) vs. user-chosen per habit (drawn in 7). My pick: per habit, default Excused.
Under the hood (for reference)Choice
StorageAn optional RoutineSchedule Codable on Routine; Habit gains optional weekdays + linkedRoutineID. All optional, so SwiftData migrates lightweight, and the backup covers both.
NotificationsA pure planner computes the next ~14 days of occurrences (reminder, nudge, heads-up) within iOS's 64-pending budget. It re-plans on launch, on foreground, on edit, and when a session starts/finishes, which is what cancels the nudge.
QRSame snappet://routine/v1/ code plus an optional sc key. Older builds ignore unknown keys (import still works; the schedule just isn't picked up).
Photo importPhotosPicker, then Vision VNDetectBarcodesRequest (QR), then the existing SharedRoutine(decoding:). Fully on-device.
TestsOccurrence math (DST, every-2-weeks, end-after-N), scheduled-day streaks, QR round-trip with/without schedule plus old-decoder compatibility, and photo decode against a rendered QR. All pure XCTest, no device needed.
