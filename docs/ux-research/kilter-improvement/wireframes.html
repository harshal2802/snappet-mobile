Kilter Improvement — your climbing record, made smart

Five upgrades to the Kilter mini-app, designed as one coherent, on-device "your climbing record" system on the Pulse Pro language: recognize the board at a gym you've been to (coarse on-device location + Bluetooth — no more re-picking layout/size/angle) · a first-class Your Climbs gallery of the climbs you set · a redesigned analytics dashboard · a grouped, filterable session history · and a new On the Board log of every climb you lit on the wall. Real SnappetColor tokens (kilter amber #F59E0B), dark-mode-first. No backend, no network, no accounts.

Flow 1 · Phase P1

Auto-detect board on arrival — location + Bluetooth

A coarse, on-device location match recognizes the place you've climbed before and suggests your usual board on arrival, before you even connect; the Bluetooth identifier then pins the exact board and disambiguates two boards at one gym. Layout + size restore automatically; angle is a one-tap confirm (it's physically mutable and never readable over BLE). First-ever board/place stays today's one-time manual pick. Built by generalizing the existing single-board memory into a KilterBoardMemory (a 1:1 clone of BandMemory) plus CoreLocation — the coarse place is stored on-device and never uploaded.

1 Arrive → location matches a known place→ 2 Suggest your usual board (pre-connect)→ 3 Connect → Bluetooth IDs the exact board→ 4 Restore layout + size · confirm angle→ 5 Manage boards + location in Settings

Delete keeps your logged ascents. No community signals (none exist on-device).

Flow 5 · Phase P5

On the Board — the climbs you lit on the wall

A brand-new axis the app can't show today: lighting a climb on the board (KilterBoardController.illuminate) is a transient BLE write recorded nowhere. We capture a lightweight lit-event at the illuminate call sites (deduped per climb-per-session so it stays bounded) and surface an On the Board timeline of every climb you actually pulled up & worked — including the ones you never formally logged — with status joined from your ascent log (Lit / Attempt / ✓ Sent) and one-tap re-light. A "Recently on the board" rail on the root resumes a project instantly.

1 Light a climb on the board (existing)→ 2 Lit-event captured (climb · grade · angle · time)→ 3 Browse On the Board timeline + status→ 4 One-tap re-light from history or root rail
