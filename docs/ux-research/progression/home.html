Home, built around your buddy

Home today is a stack of separate summaries. For people who train, the redesign puts the buddy at the top, then one clear thing to do today, then the week, your recent wins and what's next. Everything else (habits, focus, budget, climbing, the activity feed) stays, below the training section. People who don't train keep today's Home unchanged (frame 7). Only training earns XP, for now.

Same rules as the approved progression design: Level 12 · Adult, 590 / 700 XP, Form 82%, a 5-week streak, Push Day planned Mon/Thu, today is Thursday. Buddy images are real renders from the app. Uses: P1 (XP, level, Form, already built) · P2 (buddy screen, pause, freezes) · routine schedules (135–144) · records (#331).
