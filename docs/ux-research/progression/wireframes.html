Progression — XP, level, Form & your 3D buddy

The training buddy you tried on your phone, now wired to your training. Every session earns XP, and XP raises a level that never goes down. The level is what makes your buddy grow: Egg → Hatchling → Sprout → Adult → Legend. Separately, Form tracks how consistent you've been lately and shows up only as your buddy's mood. Missing sessions makes it tired, never smaller. Pause mode and streak freezes cover rest weeks, injuries and travel. The buddy images here are real renders from the prototype (frame 1 will play its cheer animation).

Built on what's already shipped: the session-detail records, streaks and milestones (#331) decide the XP bonuses; the routine schedule (135–144) decides "on plan"; the 3D creature (#332) is the buddy. Not in this pass: the Companion and Athlete 3D styles (art packs come later), Android, the Watch face.

Level, Form, pause, freezes

LevelNever goes down. Level n → n+1 costs 100 + 50·n XP: Level 10 ≈ 25 sessions, Level 20 ≈ 85 (about 10 months at 2 a week).
StagesEgg 1 · Hatchling 2–4 · Sprout 5–9 · Adult 10–19 · Legend 20+
FormPlanned sessions done ÷ planned, over 4 weeks; skips with a reason are neutral; no schedule → compared with your usual week. Mood only.
Week streakWeeks with at least one session (or all planned sessions, if you prefer: Q4).
FreezesEarn 1 per 4-week streak, hold up to 2, used automatically on a missed week.
Pause1–4 weeks or until you're back; Form and streak are held; the buddy sleeps.

Your calls

Q1 · Your history counts? A work out XP from every past session, so you hatch straight to your real level (frame 8) · B everyone starts at Egg on update day. My pick: A. Starting a regular at Egg would feel like being reset.
Q2 · Does Form affect XP? A no, it's mood only (as drawn) · B high Form gives up to +20% XP. My pick: A. B quietly turns a bad month into slower progress, which is the punishment loop we agreed to avoid.
Q3 · XP numbers. The table above: about 120–190 XP for a normal session, a 300/day cap, Legend in about 10 months at 2 sessions a week. My pick: as drawn; the numbers live in one pure, unit-tested table, so they're easy to tune after you feel it.
Q4 · What keeps a week streak? A at least one session that week · B all your planned sessions. My pick: A; "planned" is already measured by Form, so the streak can stay simple and forgiving.
Q5 · Where it lives. Home card (9) + finish screen (1–3) + session detail (10) + widgets (11). No new tab. My pick: as drawn. The Watch and the Lock Screen come later, using the same still images.
Q6 · Build order. P1 engine (XP, levels, Form, backfill) + finish screen + Home card · P2 buddy screen, pause, freezes · P3 level-up/growth moments + session-detail slot · P4 widgets. My pick: as listed, each its own PR.
