Kilter — several boards in one place

At a gym with more than one Kilter board, today's app gets confusing. New boards are all called "Kilter board" (or "Board" plus a serial number). You can only rename one later, in Settings. Connect silently picks a board you've used before, otherwise the strongest signal, so you can end up on the neighbour's. And the "you've arrived" card guesses the board you used last. This also fixes a first-open gap: the download only keeps climbs that fit one board size. It adds four things: name a board the first time you connect, choose from a list when more than one board is in range, Flash a board's corners to see which physical wall it is, and ask which board on arrival.

Builds on PR #398 (the Kilter connect fix): it already scans and ranks boards by signal and by which ones you've used before, so this only adds the choice and the names. Unchanged: with only one board in range, Connect still connects straight away.

Your calls

Q1 · Default name when you skip naming: A size + serial number, e.g. "12 x 12 (#4821)" · B "Kilter board 1", "Kilter board 2" (as today, numbered). My pick: A. The size tells boards of different sizes apart, and the serial tells apart two of the same size.
Q2 · When to show the chooser: A whenever 2+ boards are in range (as drawn) · B only when none of them is a board you've used before. My pick: A. In a gym, the board you used last time isn't necessarily the one you're standing at.
Q4 · First open (frames A–B): list every board you climb on at download time (get climbs that fit any of them) and pick from that list on first connect. My pick: yes. It fixes the missing-climbs gap, and naming becomes one tap.
Q3 · Flash pattern: A four corners, 5 seconds (as drawn) · B the whole board pulsing. My pick: A. Easy to spot at a glance, and over quickly. Either way it replaces what's lit for those seconds.
