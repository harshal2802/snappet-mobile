Clips grid — zoom levels (Photos-style)

One pinch continuum, like Photos: 3 columns → 5 columns (months) → Year. The covers stay under your fingers and slide into each new layout as you pinch, they don't jump when you let go. Steps 1 and 2 already exist; step 3 is new — pick A or B.

How the transition behaves (all levels)

  • Follows the finger — every cover moves and resizes in step with the pinch, not after you let go.
  • Anchored — the cover under your fingers stays under them, and everything else moves around it.
  • Settles — when you let go it finishes or springs back depending on how far you pinched and how fast, with a light tap of haptic feedback when the level changes.
  • Year → tap — tapping in the year view zooms back into the month around what you tapped, like Photos.
  • The segmented control (Year · Months · All) gives the same levels without pinching, and the UI tests use it.

A vs B

  • A · Year mosaic: 9 tiny covers per row under a big year title, with small month labels. Continuous with the other levels (the covers just keep shrinking), so the pinch feels seamless. Recommended.
  • B · Year cards: one card per year (like Photos' Years tab) with a mosaic of the year's best covers. Prettier, but the pinch from Months cross-fades into cards instead of the covers flowing into place.
