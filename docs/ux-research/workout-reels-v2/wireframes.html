Workout Reels v2 — "Highlights": don't remove it, converge it

Verdict: the capability is worth keeping, the mini-app is not. Today "Workout Reels" is a separate tile whose list screen (HealthKit workouts → media in window) was just made redundant by the watch-workouts→Clips import, and whose export is the app's weakest video output: ReelViewModel.export() calls the nil-overlay path of ReelExporter, so reels ship as plain stitched footage while Studio/Feed exports burn the glass HR overlay. The proposal: retire the tile, keep ReelView/HighlightEngine as the shared "Highlight Reel" maker — reachable from any session — rendered through the Studio overlay pipeline, posting into the Clips feed, plus an auto-generated Weekly Highlight Reel (the one thing nothing else in the app can do: a cross-session, HR-ranked montage).

The tile retires; the hero re-points. The App Library's flagship hero becomes the Weekly Highlights drop (tap ▶ plays this week's reel), and the fresh-install Home CTA follows it. The RETIRED tile is shown here only for the wireframe — it ships as simply gone. Health/Photos permission priming moves from module-entry onboarding to first "Make a Highlight Reel" tap (still value-first, still just-in-time).

✕ Delete (the redundant shell)

  • Workout Reels mini-app tile + WorkoutModule — a destination with nothing exclusive left.
  • WorkoutListView — its HealthKit workout list is now a poorer copy of History + the watch-import Clips pipeline.
  • Module-scoped OnboardingView entry — permission priming moves to first reel tap.
  • Home fresh-install CTA → Workout Reels onboarding; App Library flagship hero re-points to Weekly Highlights.

✓ Keep (the machinery, all reused)

  • HighlightEngine — untouched; also powers HRV/recovery/effort elsewhere.
  • ReelView / ReelViewModel / ReelFlowPolicy — become the shared reel maker (Kilter already pushes ReelView today).
  • ReelExporter — gains nothing; Reels finally passes the hrOverlay: it already supports.
  • All recovery states (denied / limited / empty / export-failed) + FeedbackStore edit telemetry.

✦ Add (what makes it special)

  • Glass HR overlay burned into reels (Studio kit; ~1-line plumbing + picker UI).
  • Post to Clips — reels become feed posts with a ✦ REEL badge; "Reels" filter chip.
  • Weekly Highlight Reel — auto cross-session montage (gym + Kilter + watch), the capability nothing else has.
  • Format presets 9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1 via ClipAspectResolver; intensity badges on clip rows.
  • Later: beat-synced music, auto stat title/end cards, monthly "Wrapped" reel.
