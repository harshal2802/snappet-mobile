Apple Watch workouts → Clips feed

The app never controlled these workouts. After any Apple Watch workout finishes and syncs, HealthKit background-delivery wakes Snappet, mints a completed WorkoutSession anchor keyed on the HKWorkout.uuid, auto-discovers the photos/videos shot inside its window (±90 s), and posts them to Clips — only if media exists. On first install it back-fills all-time so the feed has content out of the box.
