Don’t hog the board

A Kilter board takes ONE phone at a time, and today Snappet holds it for your whole session — nobody else (Snappet or the Kilter app) can connect while you climb. Proposal: hold it only while you’re lighting climbs; after a short idle, let go. The climb stays lit; the next “light” reconnects in about a second.
