Session details — informative & gamified

Reviewed live on the simulator with four weeks of seeded history (strength, hangboard + Tindeq, bouldering, runs). Today the page describes one session in isolation. The redesign puts this session next to its routine's history: what changed since last time, the trend, the records you set, and what's next. Each workout type gets the numbers that matter for it.

Bugs found during the review (fixed regardless of design): ① heart-rate recovery prints a double minus ("−−3 bpm rec.") when HR rose after a set; ② bodyweight lifts show "Pullups · 3 sets · 0 kg"; ③ untimed climbing shows "Median time on climb 0:00"; ④ "First V5 send" is detected but never shown on the detail page; ⑤ the page title is just "Session", with no routine name; ⑥ an empty media block takes about half a screen above your sets.

Your calls

Q1 · Gamification. A records + streaks + next milestone (frame 6) · B A plus levels/XP (6B). My pick: A; B can be layered on later.
Q2 · "Compared with". Last session of this routine (shown as ▲/▼), with best ever as the dotted line and in the badges. A quick session with no routine compares with past sessions of the same type and name. My pick: as drawn.
Q3 · Bottom of the page. Heart rate as a compact row that expands, and empty media as one row (frame 5). Pages with media keep the full gallery. My pick: as drawn.
Q4 · Where else? The same "earned" badges and change-since-last-time on the finish screen right after a workout, so the payoff lands immediately, not only when you open History later. My pick: yes, since they share the same building blocks.
