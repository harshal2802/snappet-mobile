Generate — training-data check & light it here

Two additions to the Kilter Generate panel. 1 · After every generated climb, a card says whether it was in the model's training data. If not, it shows how close it is to the nearest training climb. 2 · Connect a board and light the climb right in the panel. It lights automatically each time you generate, and you don't have to save it first.

Where the data comes from: I re-ran scripts/build-climb-dataset.py (≥3 ascents, Original layout, salt kilter-gen-v1) on the catalog published on the Snappet Pages site. It reproduced the trained split exactly: 87,879 train / 10,961 val examples = 52,878 training climbs (6,600 val, 6,860 test). The script would also write a small training-index.json.gz (each climb's holds + which split it's in) next to the model on the Pages site. The app downloads it once, like the model, and checks a generated climb on the phone. This works even if your installed catalog is filtered to your board size.
