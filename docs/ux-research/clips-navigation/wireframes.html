Clips — Finding your way around

Session headers in the feed, months in the grid. On a real library (300+ posts) the feed is one long scroll with no dates, and a 6-exercise gym session is 6 near-identical headers in a row. Now each session gets a header — name, date, kind, post count — that pins to the top while you're inside that session, so you always know where you are. The grid (▦) groups covers by month with pinned month headers, which makes it the "jump to month" view: scroll to September, tap a cover, land on that post.

Nothing else changes: posts look the same, filters and search group their results the same way.
