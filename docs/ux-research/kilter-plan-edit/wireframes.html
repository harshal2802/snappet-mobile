During the session. The saved plan stays editable: swipe a pending climb to remove it, “+ Add a climb” at the end. Sent or tried climbs stay put (they’re your history).

Q1 · Reorder by dragging? Suggest: yes, within and across sections (dragging into Project makes it a project).
Q2 · Where does an added climb go? Suggest: the section whose “+ Add” you tapped; during a session, “+ Add a climb” picks the section from its grade (below your grade → warm-up, at → send, above → project).
Q3 · Edits after Adjust / Shuffle? Suggest: once edited, changing Adjust asks “Rebuild and lose your edits?” instead of silently replacing them.
