Recap — your sessions, retold

A new third tab, Recap, between Today and Apps: an Instagram-style, infinite-scroll feed of your own climbing & gym sessions as polished, shareable Pulse Pro cards — woven through with auto-generated insight & recap cards that compose themselves from whatever data you actually have. When fitness-band HR data exists it deepens the card; when photos/video exist, iOS auto-edits an HR-overlay clip; and a Spotify-Wrapped-style Story rail turns the tab into a daily-open hook. Built on a pure, eligibility-gated FeedComposer + an append-only, UUIDv5 FeedActivity log that is personal now, social-ready later. Real SnappetColor / PulseColors tokens (kilter amber #F59E0B, workout ember #FF9645, coral brand #FF7A6B), dark-mode-first. On-device only — no backend, no network, no accounts. "Share" = export an image/clip to the OS share sheet.
