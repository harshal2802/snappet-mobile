Recap Feed — scroll timestamp indicator

Problem: the infinite Recap feed has no time anchor while scrolling, so you lose your place. Three ways to fix it, in context, with real Pulse tokens. Cards are recency-bounded (≈reverse-chronological), and every card already carries an anchorDate — so the indicator just surfaces what's already there.

Floating date pill. A translucent glass capsule pinned top-center shows the bucket + exact date of the topmost card. It appears on scroll, fades ~1.5s after you stop — zero layout cost, always honest (it reflects the real card at the top, so a floated PR can't mislead). A faint right-edge progress rail hints how deep you are.
