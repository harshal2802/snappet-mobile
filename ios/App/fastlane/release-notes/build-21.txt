Your training buddy now celebrates every level and lives on your Home and Lock Screen.

LEVEL-UP MOMENTS
• Finish a session that takes you past a level and a full-screen moment opens: your buddy cheers,
  shows the new level, and how far it is to the next growth.
• Cross into a new stage (Hatchling, Sprout, Adult, Legend) and you watch your buddy grow from the old
  stage into the new one, with what's new.
• Each level is celebrated once, and the levels your past sessions already earned never trigger one.
• Want a look now? Workout → Settings → Labs → Training buddy → Preview level-up / Preview growing up.

XP EVERYWHERE
• Every session's detail now starts with what it earned, e.g. "+187 XP · Level 12 · on plan · Bench PR".
• History rows show the XP each session earned.

WIDGETS
• Training buddy widgets: small and medium on the Home Screen, plus two on the Lock Screen. Your buddy,
  level, mood and streak, and what's up next with about how much XP it's worth.
• Long-press the Home Screen → ＋ → Snappet → Training buddy. They update whenever you open or leave
  the app.

KNOWN ISSUES
• Only the Creature style exists so far; Companion and Athlete are coming.
• Android doesn't have the buddy yet.

WHAT WOULD HELP MOST
• Add the small and medium widgets. Do they show the right buddy, level and streak, and update after a
  workout?
• If you level up after a session, did the moment feel right? Try the previews in Labs too.
