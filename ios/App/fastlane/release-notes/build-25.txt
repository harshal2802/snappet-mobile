A new look across the whole app, racket sports, and a much steadier Kilter Board connection.

A NEW LOOK
• Snappet has been restyled end to end: calmer screens, one card style, light big numbers, thin charts and one accent colour.
• Pick your accent in Settings → Appearance (Periwinkle, Glacier, Teal or Coral). Every screen, sheet, widget and Live Activity follows it.
• Home: a small buddy avatar, and everything on Home taps through to where it came from.
• Done: Home, Clips, Recap, Gym Tracker (dashboard, library, routines, player, finish, history, timers), Kilter, the buddy, Festival, Wardrobe, Household, Habits, Pomodoro, Journal, Tip, Budget, Split Expenses, and the widgets.

RACKET SPORTS
• Log matches and drills for tennis, padel, pickleball, badminton, squash and table tennis in a Quick Session, with heart rate back-filled from Apple Health.

KILTER BOARD
• More than one board: name them, pick which board to use, and Flash to find it.
• Connecting is more reliable (retry and auto-reconnect), and Snappet lets go of a shared board after 10 s idle.
• Lighting a climb is now on tap by default ("Light climbs automatically" in settings turns it back on).
• Filter by Not tried / Projects / Sent, and edit a planned session before or during it.
• Use your own photo of your board behind the holds.

FESTIVAL
• Your night: end a set, see what's next, breaks, and edit the lineup and set delays.

FIXES
• Budget no longer crashes when the month has spending in two categories.
• Wardrobe cards no longer squeeze "Open" and "Wear" into a sliver; Tidy counts are right.

WHAT WOULD HELP MOST
• Try each accent in Settings → Appearance. Does anything still show an old colour?
• Add the Today, Buddy and Household widgets, and start a workout or a focus session: do the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island use your accent?
• Log spending in two Budget categories. Does Budget stay open?
• Log a racket match or drill. Did the heart rate come in from Health?
