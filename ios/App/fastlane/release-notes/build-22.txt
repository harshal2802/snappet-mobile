Kilter got faster and smarter: snappy search, only climbs your board can light, and the generator now
tells you whether a climb is new.

KILTER SEARCH & FILTERS
• Typing in search and dragging the grade slider no longer lag, even with the full catalog installed.
• With the two grade thumbs close together, the grade labels no longer overlap. They merge into one
  label, e.g. "7c/V9 – 8a/V11".

ONLY CLIMBS YOUR BOARD CAN LIGHT
• Browse now lists only climbs that fit your board size, so every hold lights. Some climbs were set on a
  bigger board, like a 16 x 12, and use holes a 12 x 12 doesn't have, so a hold never lit.
• Open such a climb another way (Saved, a QR code, History) and it tells you which holds aren't on your
  board and which board it was set for.

GENERATE: IS IT REALLY NEW?
• After generating a climb, a card says whether the model's training data had it: an exact copy, a
  match with a climb the model never saw, "very close" (80% or more of holds shared), or new, with the
  nearest training climb. Tap "See it on the board" to compare.
• Light a generated climb right from the Generate panel. Connect your board there, tap Light, or turn on
  "Auto-light each new climb" to try climbs one after another at the wall. No need to save first.
• Fixed: a climb generated for a different board size than yours could light the wrong holds.

KNOWN ISSUES
• On Android, browse doesn't filter by board size yet.
• The training check downloads a one-time 4.6 MB file the first time you use the generator.

WHAT WOULD HELP MOST
• Type fast in Kilter search. Does it keep up?
• Generate a few climbs at the board with auto-light on. Did each light correctly, and did the training
  card's verdict make sense?
