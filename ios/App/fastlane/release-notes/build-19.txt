Routines can now go on a schedule, remind you, and count toward a habit, and the screen no longer
goes to sleep mid-workout.

SCHEDULE A ROUTINE  (Gym Tracker)
• Open a routine and tap Schedule. Repeat it on chosen weekdays (every week, or every 2–4 weeks
  for A/B splits), every few days, or just once. Use the same time every day or a different time
  per weekday, and optionally end it on a date or after a number of sessions.
• Reminders you control: remind at the start time or up to an hour before, a "still on for it?"
  nudge if you haven't started, and an optional heads-up the evening before. Sound and Time
  Sensitive (gets through Focus) are per routine.
• Long-press a reminder to Start now (opens the workout straight away), Snooze 15 min, or Skip
  today. Starting the routine early cancels its nudge automatically.
• Routines now opens with an Up next card, showing what's planned today and this week (done,
  planned, missed, skipped), with Start and Skip right there.

HABITS
• A scheduled routine can be tracked in Habits. Finishing the workout ticks the habit off for you,
  and several routines can share one habit (e.g. one "Gym" habit fed by Push, Pull and Legs).
• Streaks now count scheduled days only, so a Mon/Wed/Fri routine keeps its streak through the
  rest days. Rest days show as dashed circles.
• Any habit can now run on specific weekdays, not just every day.
• You choose whether "Skip today" keeps the streak alive (Excused, the default) or breaks it.
• A linked habit shows today's session with a Start button that opens the workout.
• Home and the Today widget no longer count a habit as "left to do" on its day off.

SHARE & SCAN ROUTINES
• A routine's QR code can now carry its schedule (Include schedule, on by default). Your Habits
  data is never shared.
• Routines ＋ now offers Scan QR Code and Import from Photos, so a screenshot of a code someone sent
  you works too. Everything is read on your phone.
• When you import, you can keep the schedule, change it, or leave it off. Tracking it in Habits is
  off unless you turn it on.

SCREEN STAYS ON DURING WORKOUTS
• The screen no longer locks during rests, between sets, or after a timed set. It stays on while
  the workout is open and locks normally once you minimize it.
• Workout → Settings → Keep screen on: Whole workout (default), Only timers, or Off.

KNOWN ISSUES
• Reminders plan two weeks ahead and refresh each time you open the app, so if you don't open
  Snappet for over two weeks, reminders pause until you do.
• Android doesn't have schedules, Habits links or QR scanning yet.

WHAT WOULD HELP MOST
• Schedule a routine a few minutes out and try Start now / Snooze / Skip today from the
  notification. Did each do what you expected?
• Share a scheduled routine to another phone (in-app scan, the Camera app, or a screenshot via
  Import from Photos). Did the schedule come through?
• During a workout, did the screen ever lock when you didn't want it to?
