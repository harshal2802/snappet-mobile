Hangboard protocols, the Tindeq force sensor, session history, and a 3D training buddy that grows
with every workout.

HANGBOARD PROTOCOLS  (Gym Tracker)
• Ready-made Max hangs, Endurance repeaters, Contact and Abrahangs, or build your own: hang time,
  rest, reps, sets, and "until I tap done" reps for Contact-style holds. Put them in any routine.
• Added load: a weight on your harness, or a pulley taking weight off. Type any value, not just
  ±2.5. One hand, or one hand each side, with the hand recorded per set.
• Adjust a protocol mid-run, then keep the change or use it once.
• Schedules can now repeat any number of times a day, down to every minute.

TINDEQ PROGRESSOR  (Workout → Settings → Force sensor)
• Pair it once and zero it. During a hang you see live force against a target band; reps start
  and stop on their own when you load and let go. Set targets as a % of your max, measure your
  max with the Max pull test, and see peak force per rep afterwards. Connect mid-workout too.

SESSION DETAILS
• A finished session now shows where it sits in its routine's history: "8th Push Day", what
  changed since last time, records and firsts it earned, your week streak, a progress chart and
  the next milestone. Strength compares each exercise with last time; hangboard shows force per
  hang; climbing compares your grade pyramid with the last 30 days.
• Fixes: a double minus in HR recovery, bodyweight sets showing "0 kg", "Median time on climb
  0:00", and 67.5 kg showing as 68 kg.

YOUR TRAINING BUDDY  (new)
• Every session earns XP: finishing, minutes, training on plan, records, milestones and your
  week streak, up to 300 a day. XP raises a level that never goes down, and your 3D buddy grows
  with it: Egg → Hatchling → Sprout → Adult → Legend. Your past sessions already count; hatch it
  from Home.
• Form (how consistent you've been lately) shows only as its mood. A missed week makes it tired,
  never smaller.
• Pause mode for injury, travel, illness or a rest week: Form and your streak are held, routine
  reminders can go quiet, and your buddy sleeps.
• Streak freezes: every 4 weeks of streak earns one (hold up to 2), used automatically on a week
  you miss.
• The finish screen shows what each session earned, line by line.
• Once you've earned XP, Home leads with your buddy, today's planned session and what it's worth,
  the week in XP, recent wins and what's coming up. Your other apps sit below.

KNOWN ISSUES
• Only the Creature style exists so far; Companion and Athlete are coming.
• Android doesn't have hangboard protocols, the Tindeq or the buddy yet.

WHAT WOULD HELP MOST
• Hatch your buddy. Does the level it hatches to feel fair for the training you've logged?
• Finish a real session. Does the XP breakdown match what you did?
• Try a Tindeq hang with auto reps. Did reps start and stop when you expected?
• Start a pause with quiet reminders. Did a scheduled reminder stay silent?
